# Haswell single-thread f64 matrix multiplication

[Issue 208](https://github.com/tensor4all/colmat-rs/issues/208) measures the time for callers of colmat's public matrix multiplication. SVD also makes internal GEMM calls. A separate trace of square SVDs at orders 511, 513 and 1024 identified both their logical shapes and the leading dimensions of their views. For example, the 1024 decomposition calls a 513 × 1025 × 513 product with all three leading dimensions equal to 1032. Measuring that layout separately matters because it changes cache access.

## Experiments

- Unrolling the existing 12 × 4 kernel four depth steps at a time saved about 2–3% on the three primary squares. It lost time on the 64-square scan. Its full output matched the old kernel bit-for-bit. The final candidate restores the original general kernel and specializes only the measured Haswell family. [First comparison](https://github.com/tensor4all/t4a-private-gemm-x86/pull/9#issuecomment-7001457667)
- Keeping the old driver and using an 8 × 6 tile with fixed-offset loads improved the 1024-square median from 64.11 to 59.12 ms in a four-round scan. System OpenBLAS took 50.11 ms. On the plain 8 × 6 variant, changing only the depth cap from 512 to 256 moved 1024 from 58.52 to 56.08 ms in a separate three-round scan. These probes motivated changing how the microkernel receives and reuses its input.
- The selected driver packs both inputs. A tile uses twelve YMM accumulators, two vectors of A and two alternating broadcasts of B. Four ordered depth steps share a branch. A and B micropanels together occupy up to two thirds of L1. A row block takes up to half of L2. On the measured 32 KiB / 256 KiB machine this gives a depth cap of 192 and row blocks of 80 for full panels. The same calculation applies to rectangular and odd inputs. Padded edge microtiles use the same FMA loop and copy back only the valid output entries.
- The fast path runs inside the existing backend with its reusable thread-local buffers. It accepts full, single-thread, regular column-major f64 products on the Haswell family above the packing threshold. Indexed, triangular, diagonal and other execution modes use the general driver. Cache blocking changes where partial sums are rounded. Cross-version bitwise identity with the former 512-depth accumulation is not promised.

## Verification

The public comparison uses identical fresh inputs within each round, separate processes, a 250 ms warmup and five timed calls. The system LP64 reference is OpenBLAS 0.3.26 and the Julia ILP64 reference is 0.3.30. Each public GEMM checks the entire output against the system reference. Each full SVD checks three reconstruction entries. The focused kernel test checks every output entry exactly on dyadic inputs with row and column scale factors of 2⁻⁴⁵⁰ and 2⁴⁵⁰. It exercises multi-panel depths, Add and Replace, odd edges and padded leading dimensions. Another test forces both scratch panels to the minimum tile capacity to exercise the column blocking boundary.

The measurements use colmat 0ad25fe1, the old kernel at 8224897 and Rust 1.96 with a Haswell target. The Xeon E5-2699 v3 ran at one thread on CPU 8, with the observer on CPU 0. Both logical CPUs of the measured physical core passed idle checks at both ends of the run. Turbo was enabled and the last-level cache was shared. These are local reference measurements for a draft. The separate controlled merge-acceptance campaign is still outstanding.

## Outcome

In eight matched rounds the packed public GEMM reduced time relative to baseline by 9.3% at 511³, 11.3% at 513³ and 16.8% at 1024³ (median within-round reductions, seven wins each). Relative to system OpenBLAS, the median within-round time gaps were 1.3%, 4.6% and 4.6%. For Julia's build they were 1.1%, 4.9% and 3.8%. The exploratory 2048-square cell used 20.2% less time than baseline. The full SVD saved 0.9%, 1.0% and 2.6% in four matched rounds. One NT accumulate control was 6.0% slower in this scan despite taking the general kernel. The [draft PR](https://github.com/tensor4all/t4a-private-gemm-x86/pull/9) carries the complete cells, raw timings and reproduction driver.
